# YonerAI AI Continuity Manifesto

## Intelligence may change  People must not be reset

**Published:** July 29  2026 JST  
**Author:** YoneRai12  Founder of YonerAI  
**Project status:** Founder-led  pre-incorporation project in Japan  
**Document status:** Public position and design commitment  not a production-completeness claim

AI is moving beyond answering questions.

It is beginning to remember people  perform work  operate other systems  and act on the world.

The defining question of this era is not only which model becomes the most intelligent.

**When AI knows you deeply  who owns its memory and authority  you or the AI company?**

As AI becomes more useful  people will entrust more of their lives to it.

Not only conversations.

Identity  Memory  Relationships  Permissions  Workflows  Files  Tools  Agent Configuration  Accumulated Knowledge  and World State.

When these are fixed inside one model  one cloud  or one company  personalization becomes a form of lock-in.

The ability to change models is not enough.

**Freedom means changing models without losing yourself.**

## Open weights are necessary  but not sufficient

Open weights matter.

The ability to download  inspect  modify  and run models on infrastructure you control is a foundation for competition  security  sovereignty  affordability  and innovation.

But model portability alone does not make a person's AI life portable.

A model may move to local infrastructure while Identity  Memory  Permissions  Workflows  Relationships  and Accumulated Knowledge remain trapped in a service.

> **Open weights make models portable.**
>
> **YonerAI is being built to make human AI continuity portable.**

## What must remain on the human side

Models may change.

Providers  clouds  devices  and interfaces may change.

The following must remain with the person or organization they serve.

1. **Identity**  Who the AI serves.
2. **Memory**  What it remembers  forgets  and treats as evidence.
3. **Authority**  Who grants permission  who can stop it  and who holds final judgment.
4. **Workflow**  How work  creation  learning  and daily life continue.
5. **World Model**  How people  organizations  digital systems  and physical reality are understood and updated.
6. **Evidence**  What was read  proposed  executed  and changed.
7. **Portability**  Whether continuity survives a change of model  environment  or operator.

## The YonerAI position

YonerAI is not being built only to create one strongest model.

It is being built as a Provider Independent AI Operating System that connects different models  agents  applications  devices  clouds  local environments  organizations  digital systems  and the physical world to a continuing human-side Identity  Memory  Authority  and World Model.

It is also intended to become an AI Native SaaS Foundation so that every new AI application does not have to rebuild memory  permissions  approvals  auditing  model routing  and rollback from the beginning.

YonerAI is not merely a model router.

> **One Identity.**
>
> **One Memory.**
>
> **One World Model.**
>
> **One Continuous System.**

This does not mean concentration inside one company.

It means continuity that remains with each human or organization across multiple forms of intelligence and execution.

## Design commitments

### 1  Provider independence

Providers must be replaceable.

Cloud models  open-weight models  local models  and specialized models should be selectable according to purpose  capability  cost  privacy  and risk.

### 2  Memory sovereignty

Memory must be treated as a human-side asset  not an accessory owned by a model.

Memory should carry provenance  validity  revision history  visibility  and approval state  with mechanisms for Diff  Why  and Rollback.

### 3  Human authority

AI must not grant itself additional authority.

Reading  writing  sending  publishing  paying  and changing infrastructure must remain distinct capabilities.

High-consequence transitions belong under human approval.

### 4  The same experience across cloud  local  and hybrid

Cloud  local  self-hosted  and hybrid operation should not become disconnected products.

They should be different execution environments under the same Identity  Memory  Policy  and Audit contract.

### 5  Evidence  audit  and rollback

AI that acts on the world must be traceable.

Important execution should leave evidence  verify resulting changes  and support stopping  isolation  recovery  and rollback.

### 6  Open weights as a first-class path

Open weights should be treated as a first-class path for users  organizations  and researchers to control capability and data  not merely as a lower-cost substitute.

The distinct risks of irreversible release  modification  and misuse must also be addressed with safeguards tied to capability and demonstrated harm.

### 7  Governed self-evolution

AI may observe  discover problems  propose improvements  and help implement them.

Self-evolution must not leave the boundary of human Authority  Evidence  Review  Approval  and Rollback.

### 8  No false completion claims

This document is not a declaration that YonerAI is complete.

Concepts  implemented components  verified components  public components  and unimplemented capabilities must remain distinguishable.

Trust comes from evidence and boundaries  not marketing language.
